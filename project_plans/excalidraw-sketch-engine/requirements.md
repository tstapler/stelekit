# Requirements: excalidraw-sketch-engine

**Date**: 2026-11-14
**Type**: feature addition (extends the sketch layer from `project_plans/home-project-sketching`)
**Complexity**: 4 — cross-cutting: new drawing/scene model, constraint solver, renderer and gesture layer across all KMP targets, plus file-format interop and a persistence migration

Interview skipped: the request and the existing code answer every required information goal; remaining unknowns are in Open Questions for Phase 2.

## Problem Statement
SteleKit's sketch layer (`kmp/.../sketch/`, `ui/sketch/`) is a dimension-centric engineering tool: primitives are only line/rect with integer coordinates in sixteenths of a sheet unit (`SketchPrimitive` in `model/SketchModels.kt`), plus zones, named dimensions and sum/equal constraints. It cannot express free-form design or architecture diagrams (shapes with text, arrows bound to shapes, freehand, images, frames), has a thin tool set, and offers no layered experience beyond named layers. The owner wants Excalidraw's feature baseline for design and architecture diagramming and FreeCAD Sketcher-style geometric constraints, in one engine whose logic is shared by every target: it runs on Desktop and Android, and, conditionally, on Web (owner decision D-104, Option W: only in a browser whose OPFS verdict is Available after Spike 0.1.8 and only while the keep or drop rule keeps Option W, otherwise the web build stays classic-only); iOS compiles but is compile-only and has no sketch file storage in this release, so iOS keeps the classic editor.

The engine serves two distinct jobs; they share one scene but have different defaults, and success is judged separately:
- **Job 1, free-form diagramming**: architecture and design diagrams (labelled boxes, bound arrows, frames, groups, hand-drawn style). Constraints are off by default. Named scenario: the 15-element architecture diagram (metric 2).
- **Job 2, technical constraint sketching**: dimensioned geometry with exact relationships. Named scenario **"bracket plate"**: four lines drawn as a closed outline, made coincident at the corners, two Horizontal and two Vertical, the lower-left corner Fixed, width Distance 6 in and height Distance 4 in, plus a circle of Radius 1/2 in whose centre is Distance 1 in from the left edge and from the bottom edge; the status reads "Fully constrained" (0 DOF), dragging any corner moves nothing (all geometry is fully determined), and a conflicting extra Parallel is rejected with the conflict named. Constraints apply to technical geometry only (line, rect, polyline, circle, arc, point; a circle is an ellipse with equal radii, and an ellipse with unequal radii is not constrainable, ADR-003 decision 5), never to text, arrows or images.

**Where the falsifiable hypotheses live**: this statement claims no number. The two claims that could be wrong are A1 (the owner will author diagrams in-app; tested by the Task 0.1.7c pre-check, the A1 gate and metric 8) and A6 (the owner has Job 2 work; tested by Task 0.1.2d), each with a numeric bar under "Risky assumptions", "A1 go/no-go gate" and "A6 go/no-go".

## Baseline
- Create line/rect primitives with snap and typed dimensions, zone constraints, named layers, SVG + CSV export, journal embeds (PRs #614, #620, #650, #663, #671).
- Diagrams are drawn outside the app (Excalidraw, Mermaid) and pasted in as images; they are not editable in SteleKit.
- No arrows, ellipses, text elements, freehand, connectors, grouping, alignment tools, or geometric constraints (coincident, parallel, tangent, etc.).
This baseline is the Phase 6 regression anchor: every existing sketch capability and stored project must keep working.

## Users / Consumers
- The owner on desktop, Android phone/foldable, and web; occasionally iOS (iOS: the classic editor only in this release, compile-only).
- Downstream: journal/page embeds of sketches; the existing SVG/CSV export and project-bridge/LLM digest path, which consume sketch dimensions.

## Success Metrics
Tolerances below are INFERRED starting values (marked `[tunable]`); the Phase 0 spikes (0.1.2, 0.1.3) may tighten or loosen them, and any change is recorded in the owning ADR, never silently. Baselines are the measured or stated state today.

1. **Tool parity (frozen inventory)**: the v1 tool inventory is exactly these 14 tools: **select, hand/pan, rectangle, diamond, ellipse, line/polyline, arrow, freedraw, text, image, eraser, frame, point, arc** (point and arc are technical-geometry tools, OD-8; if OD-8 is declined the inventory is the first 12). Excalidraw's laser pointer, embeddable, lasso and magic-frame tools are explicitly NOT in v1. The wider feature baseline (style panel, arrange, align/distribute, library) is the pinned, counted 41-item checklist in "Excalidraw feature baseline" below, enforced by `FeatureBaselineCompletenessTest`. Each has a passing scene-engine scenario test, enforced by `ToolInventoryCompletenessTest`. Baseline: 2 primitive kinds (line, rect), no tool registry.
2. **Diagram authoring (free-form job)**: the owner can draw the representative 15-element architecture diagram (9 labelled boxes, 4 bound arrows that follow moved shapes, 1 frame, 1 group) in-app. Bounds: (a) a scripted scenario completes in **at most 60 user actions** (one click, tap or key chord, a menu choice, or one typed string with its confirming key, the ONE counting convention of ux.md section 20; budget derived from UX-01, UX-02, UX-03: 9 boxes x 4 + 4 arrows x 3 + frame 2 + group 2 = 52, plus 8 slack) on JVM and Robolectric; (b) manual check on desktop and Pixel_Fold AVD: authoring time on the owner's device, first attempt, **at most 1.5x the time the owner needs for the same diagram in excalidraw.com** (advisory, owner-run). The excalidraw.com time is a MEASURED baseline, not an assumption: Task 0.1.7a (owner: Tyler, wave W1) has the owner time the same 15-element diagram in excalidraw.com on desktop and on the phone, first attempt, 3 runs, and records the median in `implementation/baselines.md`; the 1.5x bar is computed from that recorded number, and until it exists the bar is "unmeasured" rather than silently met. Baseline: not possible in-app today; the workaround is drawing in Excalidraw and pasting an image that is not editable.
3. **Constraint sketching (technical job)**: a FreeCAD-style 2D constraint set (coincident, horizontal/vertical, parallel/perpendicular, equal, tangent, distance/angle/radius dimensions, fixed) solves deterministically. Numeric bars: after a successful solve the **maximum constraint residual is below 1e-6 scene units** (1 unit = 1/16 inch) `[tunable]` over randomised property tests (the solver's own stop tolerance is 1e-9 relative to sketch scale, deliberately tighter than the 1e-6 acceptance bar: a solve that stops with a residual above 1e-6 is reported `NoConvergence` and never committed as solved); solve time for 100 constraints is under 50 ms on JVM and under 150 ms on wasmJs (hard), 50 ms on wasmJs advisory (D-30); the same input yields bit-identical output on one target and results within 1e-6 scene units across JVM and wasmJs, judged only between two targets whose `SolverTier` is `Full` (when the tiers differ, the comparison is restricted to the constraint kinds both tiers expose, the `GeometricOnly` set; a `Hidden` target is not compared; the `cross-target-agreement` block below lists every pair of tiers); over-/under-constrained sketches are classified (`Redundant`, `Conflict`, `NoConvergence`, DOF count equal to the analytic value). Named acceptance scenario: **"bracket plate"** (Job 2 below), fully constrained to 0 DOF. **Under the geometric-only fallback** (per target, on a target whose `SolverTier` is `GeometricOnly`; Abort and pivot criteria: Coincident, Horizontal, Vertical, Equal and Distance only; Fixed, Radius and Parallel are hidden) every numeric bar above stays and the named scenario becomes **"fallback plate"**, which that set CAN pass: four lines closed by Coincident at the corners, two Horizontal, two Vertical, width Distance 6 in and height Distance 4 in; plus a square pocket of four more lines (Coincident, Horizontal, Vertical, Equal on two adjacent sides, Distance 2 in on one). The status reads the analytic DOF count, 4 (2 for the plate's position, 2 for the pocket's), not "Fully constrained"; dragging a plate corner moves the whole plate with 6 x 4 in intact within 1e-6; a second Distance on the plate's bottom edge with another value is rejected naming the conflict ("Distance conflicts with Distance on Line 1"); editing the width from 6 in to 7 in widens the plate by 1 in in one undo step (`FallbackPlateScenarioTest`, Story 4.1.3). The "Fully constrained" claim for the bracket plate then waits until Fixed, Radius and Parallel each pass their randomised property test (Story 4.1.3); metric 3 is judged on the fallback plate in the meantime, and nothing in that substitution needs an owner amendment (D-60). Baseline: sum/equal/offset only, no geometric constraints.
4. **Cross-platform engine**: the scene model, command/undo engine, hit-testing and constraint solver live in `commonMain` and are tested once in `commonTest`; the same scenario fixtures render the same within this pixel tolerance on JVM, Android (Robolectric, if Spike 0.1.3e finds it reliable) and wasmJs: **at most 0.5% of pixels differ by more than 16/255 in any channel, and every differing pixel lies within 2 px of a stroke edge** `[tunable]`; CJK text is excluded from cross-target goldens (policy of Spike 0.1.3b). Reference/baseline: the JVM render of each fixture is the golden; the draw list itself must be equal across targets (exact). **If Spike 0.1.3e finds Robolectric unreliable** for Compose text and paths, the Android claim changes (it is not dropped): Android must produce a draw list EQUAL to the JVM one (exact, headless) and its pixels are checked on the Pixel_Fold by the owner against the JVM golden at this same tolerance (Task 6.2.4c); the Android column of this metric is then reported "owner-checked", never "CI-verified". Baseline today: no v2 render exists to compare.
5. **Layered UX**: layers can be created, reordered, locked, hidden, and given opacity; element z-order, grouping and frames work; undo/redo covers all of them. Named scenario **"floor plan layers"**: a sheet with 3 layers (Walls, Furniture, Notes) holding 12 elements, on which the user performs 7 operations: create a layer, reorder Walls above Furniture, lock Walls, hide Notes, set Furniture opacity to 50%, group 3 elements, and bring the group to front. **Numeric bar**: each of the 7 operations takes at most 3 actions (at most 21 in total) and is exactly one undo step (undo restores the exact previous scene, redo reapplies it), and `UxOrganisationAcceptanceTest` passes 7 of 7 (100%) on JVM and Robolectric; any operation above 3 actions or needing 2 undos is a defect against Story 3.5.2 or 3.5.1, not a tolerance. Baseline: named layers with visible and lock toggles only (no reorder, opacity, groups or frames, so 3 of the 7 operations exist today (create a layer, lock Walls, hide Notes) and the other 4 (reorder, opacity, group, bring the group to front) do not).
6. **No regression and no data loss**: every sketch stored by the current app opens, edits and exports unchanged (golden migration test against fixtures from the current schema); `MigrationRunnerSchemaSyncTest` stays green. Baseline: the list and count of existing sketch tests and golden fixtures at a RECORDED PRE-CHANGE COMMIT SHA (an ancestor of `origin/main`, so a squash merge of the engine branch cannot remove it, and fetched with full history in CI), derived by script from `git ls-tree` at that SHA (Task 0.1.7d, `scripts/sketch-baseline/record-sketch-baseline.sh`, run in W1) into the committed file `project_plans/excalidraw-sketch-engine/implementation/regression-baseline.txt`, whose first line is the SHA; Task 6.2.5a re-derives the list at that same SHA (not at HEAD) and compares (a baseline read from the working tree would already include any test added, deleted or edited by sibling tasks since); **100% of them pass unmodified** and `export_v1(converted sheet)` bytes equal `export_v1(original sheet)` for every fixture. **Platform scope (D-147)**: the golden migration and the sidecar goldens run on JVM and Android; on wasmJs, classic sketches stay database-only unless the classic file side is turned on (setting `sketch.web.classicFileSide`, default `Off`), and then `WasmClassicReconcileTest` (Task 1.4.7n) is the metric 6 evidence for the wasm reconcile (an existing web database plus a desktop-synced bundle of the same slug, divergent edits, the database overwrite of a pull); it is dropped with the rest of Story 1.4.7 when that is `Skipped(opfs-spike)`, and until it is green "the same `ProjectSidecarStore` code runs unchanged on wasm" is INFERRED.
7. **Interop**: `.excalidraw` JSON import and export round-trips the supported element subset (property test: export(import(x)) preserves element kinds, geometry, text, bindings and z-order for the supported subset; geometry compared exactly because 1 Excalidraw px = 1 scene unit); SVG and PNG export render all new element kinds. Baseline/comparison: 0 `.excalidraw` support today; the bar is 100% of the six hand-authored fixtures (five scenes plus one legacy `{focus,gap}` file) PLUS at least **N = 5 real excalidraw.com exports supplied by the owner** (owner: Tyler, Task 0.1.5d, due by the end of W1; at least one with an image, one with bound arrows and one with a frame). ## Constraints
- KMP/Compose Multiplatform, Arrow `Either`, SQLDelight + `MigrationRunner`, `DatabaseWriteActor`, bounded reads (CLAUDE.md). No new unbounded repository reads.
- Engine logic in `commonMain` (pure Kotlin, no JVM-only APIs; wasmJs compatible). Platform code only for input devices and file pickers.
- Rendering uses Compose Canvas / custom renderers; avoid heavy third-party dependencies (precedent: render-all-markdown memory). Any dependency needs an ADR and must support all targets.
- Writes go through existing writers; sketches embedded in pages obey the cross-graph write rules.
- Android persisted-log debugging; `Logger` not `android.util.Log`; uncaught-throwable guards on long-lived scopes.
- Existing integer sixteenths coordinate model is load-bearing for the dimension/fusion pipeline; the new model must coexist with it, not break it.

## Non-functional Requirements
- **Performance SLO**: 60 fps interaction (p95 frame ≤ 16 ms desktop, ≤ 24 ms on Pixel_Fold AVD) with 500 elements; hit-testing and snap queries sub-linear in element count (spatial index); constraint solve of a 100-constraint sketch < 50 ms on JVM (hard bar); on wasmJs the HARD bar is < 150 ms and 50 ms is an ADVISORY target (one figure set, owner-confirmable D-30; the same numbers appear in A2, metric 3, Spike 0.1.2 and "Abort and pivot criteria"). Frame-time rule, stated once: the CI-enforced bar is RELATIVE (500-element p95 at most 1.5x the empty-canvas p95 measured in the same run on the same host, the home-sketching precedent); the absolute 16 ms and 24 ms figures are advisory targets and are never CI checks (a host slower than the target fails neither). The absolute 16 ms and 24 ms figures are TARGETS, not measurements: Task 0.1.7b (wave W1) measures (a) the empty-canvas p95 and (b) the p95 of a throwaway Compose Canvas drawing 500 rounded rectangles while panning ("reference prototype", the cheapest thing the v2 painter could be) on the JVM harness in CI, and (c) the same two figures on the Pixel_Fold AVD (owner-run, advisory), and writes them into `benchmarks/budgets/sketch-engine-budgets.json` as `reference` entries. The Pixel_Fold figure is therefore measured, not assumed: if the measured reference-prototype p95 on the AVD already exceeds 24 ms the 24 ms target is restated from the measurement (and the abort/pivot rule for 500-element Canvas performance below applies) rather than carried forward as a promise.
- **Scalability**: ≤ 5,000 elements per scene; ≤ 200 constraints per sketch; undo history bounded. The measured performance bars above are at 500 elements and 100 constraints, so the caps are covered differently, stated exactly: at 5,000 elements the bar is behavioural (the large-drawing degradation order of Story 2.2.3 engages at 4,000 and the cap refuses new elements, tested by `architectureScenario_should_RefuseNewElement_When_SceneAtCapacity` and the Story 2.2.3 degradation tests) and the 5,000-element p95 frame time is RECORDED in `sketch-engine-budgets.json` as advisory (`sketch.frame_5000_p95_ms`); at 200 constraints the 200-constraint solve time is RECORDED as advisory (`sketch.solve_200_ms`) beside the 100-constraint hard bar, with no pass or fail figure until the Spike 0.1.2 report has measured it (gap named: no bar is invented for it).
- **Security classification**: internal/confidential (user diagrams may hold architecture details). No outbound transmission added; imported `.excalidraw` files are untrusted input (size caps, no external image fetch, no script execution).
- **Data residency**: local-first, no special requirements.
- **Accessibility**: non-drag placement mode and keyboard-only operation preserved and extended to new tools; semantic labels for elements.

## Scope
### In Scope
- Excalidraw-baseline element types and tools (shapes, arrows with binding, text, freehand, images, frames, groups, eraser, pan/zoom, selection/transform/rotate, multi-select, copy/paste/duplicate, align/distribute, z-order, lock, style panel: stroke, fill, width, dash, opacity, fonts, arrowheads, roundness), grid and snapping, object snapping, undo/redo, keyboard shortcuts, touch and stylus input, library of reusable shapes.
- Layered experience: layers panel with order/visibility/lock/opacity, frames, groups.
- FreeCAD Sketcher-style 2D geometric constraint engine and dimensional constraints, with constraint status display (DOF, conflicts, redundancy).
- Persistence: new scene schema with migration of existing `SketchPrimitive` data; SQLDelight + `MigrationRunner` entries; sidecar bundle update.
- Export: SVG, PNG, `.excalidraw` import/export; existing SVG/CSV export retained; embedding in journals/pages continues.
- Cross-platform input handling (mouse, touch, stylus, keyboard) for Desktop, Android and Web; iOS compiles (compile-only, no device verification, no sketch file storage).

### Out of Scope
- Real-time multi-user collaboration and cloud sharing (Excalidraw's live rooms, end-to-end encrypted links).
- Full 3D / parametric solid modelling (FreeCAD Part Design, assemblies); Sketcher is 2D only.
- AI "text to diagram" generation, Mermaid-to-canvas conversion (candidates for follow-up).
- Replacing the dimension-fusion/project-bridge pipeline; DXF/STEP interchange.
- Pixel-identical reproduction of Excalidraw's rough.js hand-drawn style unless the owner opts in (Open Question 3).

## Rabbit Holes
- **Constraint solver**: a general nonlinear solver (FreeCAD uses planegcs with DogLeg/LM/BFGS) in pure Kotlin is a project of its own; scope and numerical robustness (singular Jacobians, redundant constraints, solve stability under drag) need an explicit spike.
- **Coordinate model**: sixteenths-integer vs floating-point scene coordinates; rational dimensions vs solver doubles; rounding on round-trip.
- **Arrow binding and routing**: bound endpoints, elbow routing, and re-binding on edit have many edge cases.
- **Text**: multi-line editing, font metrics and wrapping differ per platform; cross-platform measurement determinism for identical rendering.
- **Freehand**: stroke smoothing/pressure and file size.
- **Rough/hand-drawn rendering** on wasmJs and iOS Canvas.
- **Hit-testing curves, rotation, and groups** at scale; spatial index choice.
- **.excalidraw fidelity**: format versioning, binary file maps (images), elements we do not support (embeddables, frames-clip details).
- **Undo semantics** across solver-moved geometry and multi-element commands.
- **wasmJs and iOS limits** on new commonMain code and input events.

## Alternatives Considered
- Embed Excalidraw itself via WebView/JS: REJECTED (ADR-007). Facts, with the confidence labels of `research/build-vs-buy.md` section 1 (open that file for the sources): (1) VERIFIED, no WebView dependency exists today: `kmp/build.gradle.kts` lines 86-163 declare no WebView, so one would be a new dependency on every target; (2) SEARCH, the KMP WebView wrappers found (compose-webview, ComposeNativeWebview) are young and their wasmJs and iOS support claims are unverified, and on wasmJs the app would embed a second web app inside Compose-in-browser; (3) Excalidraw has no constraint solver, so metric 3 is impossible inside it, and its scene is not reachable from `commonMain` for the dimension/fusion pipeline, undo integration or SQLDelight persistence without a JS bridge, which breaks the constraint "engine in commonMain"; (4) it needs a JS bundle shipped offline on every platform. Gap named, not smoothed: no bundle size, WebView start-up time or frame rate was measured, because (1) to (3) fail hard requirements rather than soft preferences, so a measurement could not change the verdict; it remains a throwaway behavioural reference in a browser only.
- Port tldraw/Excalidraw logic wholesale: licensing (MIT for Excalidraw, tldraw is not) and architecture mismatch; use as a behavioural reference only.
- Wrap an existing KMP drawing library: to be surveyed in research; none known to cover constraints.
- Extend only the existing line/rect model incrementally: cheapest, but cannot carry text, bindings or curves cleanly; likely migrate to a richer element model with a compatibility adapter.
- Constraint solver: port planegcs algorithm vs. a smaller purpose-built Gauss-Newton/LM solver vs. embedding a JVM-only library (rejected: must run on all targets).

## Flag modes (ADR-005 amendment; plan Tasks 1.4.3d, 1.4.3e, 1.4.6h)
`sketch.engine.v2` is a per-graph setting with three values, not a boolean. It is stored as a string per graph id beside the other per-graph accessors of `ProjectBridgeSettings` (read with `getString` only; a missing, blank or unknown value reads as `Off`); the key has never shipped, so there is no stored boolean to migrate:

| Mode | New sketch is created as | v1 sheets | v2 sheets | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Off` (default) | classic (v1) | classic, editable, no chip | read-only through the v2 renderer (`ReadOnly` banner) | nothing |
| `NewProjectsOnly` | v2 only in a `NewSketchProject` (the canonical sentence below); classic anywhere else, with the `ClassicEditor` toast | classic, editable, NO Upgrade chip, never converted | editable | `ConversionReady` and `V2StorageAvailable` |
| `On` | v2 in any project, after the first-write dialog; classic only where `HasTechnicalData` refuses or the user chose it | classic with the Upgrade chip; converted only by the explicit Upgrade action | editable | `ConversionReady`, `ConversionOffered` and `V2StorageAvailable` |

**NewSketchProject (canonical sentence):** A `NewSketchProject` is a project created by the very create action that adds the sketch, or an existing project that holds at least one sheet, every sheet of which classifies as v2 from its FILE (the database schema mirror is only a hint) and whose sheet list is complete (the sheet query reports no further page); an existing project with no sheets, with any v1 or unclassifiable sheet, or with more sheets than one query page does not qualify, because an older build opens it today.

Two build-time constants and one runtime platform capability, no others. `ConversionReady` (test-derived, plan glossary) is true only while every story that makes a WRITTEN v2 sheet safe to read is registered (1.1.3, 1.1.4, 1.1.5, 1.4.5): it gates BOTH v2 modes, because a brand-new v2 sheet must reach fusion, digest and previews exactly like a converted one. `ConversionOffered` is a release decision (default true; false in a release shipped after an A4 NO-GO, recorded in ADR-005): it gates only conversion, the `On` mode and the Upgrade chip. The third cap is the platform, not a build decision (D-104, D-116): `V2StorageAvailable` is `store != null && store.isAvailable()`, where the store is `ProjectBridgeDeps.sidecarStore()` and `isAvailable()` is true when `AtomicFileWriter.mode` of the sidecar root is not UNSUPPORTED. It is true on desktop and Android; true on wasmJs only in a browser whose OPFS verdict is Available (Option W: the wasm `PlatformFileSystem` then reports `supportsTempRenameWrites` true); false on a browser whose verdict is Unavailable; false on iOS; and **false when the store is null** (`GraphContentViewModelSetup.kt:221-227` returns null for a demo graph and when no graph is active, so there is no project to hold a v2 sheet; null is never "available"). With it false every mode behaves as `Off`, and the mode control is disabled with the reason of its case (`SketchReasonText.V2StorageUnavailable`, one entry per case): iOS "The new editor can't save sketches on iOS yet.", a browser that cannot save them one text per `Unavailable` reason and per browser family (the table "Web storage reasons" of ux.md 14.1 is the one source), a null store "The new editor isn't available in the demo graph or while no graph is open." (see "Web first release: what works"). The effective mode is the stored mode capped by the build and the platform; a capped option is shown disabled with "Not available in this build yet", never hidden. The read-only banner's [Use new] sets `NewProjectsOnly` (the smallest mode that makes the sheet editable and starts no conversion); choosing "All sketches (Upgrade)" is a separate, deliberate Settings action. Every mode that can write v2 shows the whole-project old-version warning before it is stored (`SketchConsentCopy`, UX-76).

## Flag and legacy-editor exit criteria
The flag `sketch.engine.v2` ships default `Off` (ADR-005; modes below). Its default mode flips from `Off` to the row's ceiling (`On` where the ceiling is `On`: new sketches are created as v2 and conversion stays an explicit per-sheet action; `NewProjectsOnly` where the combined-outcomes row caps it there: A4 NO-GO, PIVOT "import/export first", PIVOT "Job 2 first" under A4 NO-GO), and the classic editor may later be retired, only against these thresholds, read from the local `QualitySignals` counters (`sketch.quality`, plan Task 6.2.3b) and the owner's data-loss log (`implementation/quality-signals.md`, one line per report). They are owner-scale counts (one user, a few devices), INFERRED starting values and `[tunable]` like the other figures.

| Signal | Source | To flip the default (to `On`, or to `NewProjectsOnly` on a capped row) | Stop-ship (flag back OFF) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data-loss reports (a sketch's content lost, truncated or unreadable after a v2 write, sync, conversion or restore, confirmed against the backup or file) | owner log | 0 confirmed in the 4 weeks before the flip, each root-caused and covered by a regression test | any confirmed report: flag OFF, release halted until a regression test exists, restore offered |
| v2 editor crash rate | `crashes / v2Sessions` (uncaught Throwables read from `last-crash.txt` and desktop logs) | with n v2 sessions, n at least 50 and at most floor(n / 100) crashes: that is 0 crashes in 50 to 99 sessions, at most 1 in 100 to 199 | 2 or more crashes among the last 20 sessions: flag OFF (one crash never stops ship: it is root-caused and gets a regression test, and counts toward the flip rule) |
| `NoConvergence` rate | `noConvergence / solves` | with n committed solves, n at least 200 and at most floor(n / 50) `NoConvergence` (4 in 200; each is surfaced to the user, never silent) | 11 or more `NoConvergence` among the last 100 solves: constraint UI hidden (the geometric-only fallback below) |
| Save conflicts | `conflicts` | informational; a rise starts a usability review | none |

**Why the `NoConvergence` bars sit so far apart** (about 2% to flip, 11 in 100 to stop ship): flipping the default is a deliberate promotion and wants a clearly healthy rate with enough solves behind it, while stop-ship hides the constraint UI and so fires only on a rate (more than one in ten) that no healthy solver shows in a run of 100; between the two the UI stays shipped and opt-in. Both figures are INFERRED and `[tunable]`, to be recalibrated from the first real counters.

**Insufficient data (the minimum n may never be reached).** This is a one-user product, and while A6 stands at DEFER the constraint UI is parked, so there may be no committed solves at all. The window of every threshold above runs from the window start (metric 8 defines it once) and is capped at **26 weeks**, the same cap as metric 8. (a) While the constraint UI is parked or hidden (A6 DEFER, or the fallback above) the `NoConvergence` row is **not applicable** and the flip is judged on the other rows. (b) If a minimum n of an applicable row is still unmet at 26 weeks, that row is reported "insufficient data, UNMEASURED", never as met, and the default is NOT flipped by the counters: the mode stays `Off` by default (the owner may record `NewProjectsOnly` as the default instead), or the window is extended once by the owner, in the ADR-005 index; nothing flips automatically. Said plainly: at one-user scale the minimum n of the counters is likely never reached, so the default flip is in practice the owner's recorded decision under (b), informed by whatever counters exist, not an automatic result of them. (c) Stop-ship rows have no minimum n: they read the last 20 sessions or 100 solves, or fewer if fewer exist.

**Flag default `On`** (rows with ceiling `On`, A4 GO) needs all of: the A1 gate recorded PROCEED; `ParityV1SuiteTest` and the `FlagRolloutTest` baseline at 100%; the applicable thresholds above holding with their minimum n met (or the owner's recorded insufficient-data decision); the reader-only release out and every device reader-capable (A4); and the owner's confirmation in the ADR index.

**Flag default `NewProjectsOnly`** (every row whose ceiling is `NewProjectsOnly`) needs all of: the A1 gate recorded PROCEED, or PIVOT with the option that ships this release ("import/export first", or "Job 2 first" under A4 NO-GO) recorded in its ADR; the `FlagRolloutTest` baseline at 100% and `ParityV1SuiteTest` at 100% for the behaviours a new sheet uses; the applicable thresholds above holding with their minimum n met, from the same window start and the same 26-week cap (an unmet minimum n is "insufficient data" and flips nothing); the reader-only release out (6.2.5e); and the owner's confirmation in the ADR index. The criterion "every device reader-capable (A4)" is NOT used here, because it is false by definition on an A4 NO-GO row and is not needed: `NewProjectsOnly` creates v2 only in a `NewSketchProject`, so no project an older build opens today becomes unreadable. Who decides, for both flips: the owner (Tyler), by recording the flip in the ADR-005 index; nothing flips automatically, and a flipped default shows the per-device enable confirmation (Task 1.4.6c) before the first v2 sheet is created on a device that has not acknowledged it. On a capped row the mode never advances beyond the ceiling, so the `On` criteria and the retirement criteria below do not apply there until the owner ships the ADR-001 v1-compatible projection (which sets `ConversionOffered = true`) and the row becomes an `On`-ceiling row. **Retiring the classic editor UI** needs, additionally: the default ON for at least 8 weeks (aligned with metric 8) with data-loss at 0 and the crash threshold held; every sheet in the owner's graphs either Converted or consciously `Declined` (zero undecided); and the parity gate still green. Retirement removes only the editor UI: the v1 READ path, the `SketchRenderDispatcher` v1 branch and `V1ToV2.lift` stay for good, because `Declined` sheets and un-upgraded projects exist. ## Abort and pivot criteria
- **Solver (Spike 0.1.2), decided per target (D-114)**: the result is recorded per target as a `SolverTier` (`Full`, `GeometricOnly` or `Hidden`) for JVM (which stands for desktop and Android) and for wasmJs, because only one criterion is target-dependent. **Hard bars and advisory figures, stated once** (the block below; `check-plan.py` parses it and the plan's thresholds must equal its hard rows): a hard bar decides a tier; an advisory figure never selects a tier by itself, a miss is recorded in `baselines.md` and shown to the owner, who may then record Android as its own tier by re-recording D-114. The target-independent criteria (residual under 1e-6 scene units, correct `Redundant`/`Conflict` classification on the fixtures, no branch flip on the 200-step drag, no NaN on the fuzz corpus) are one code path: a miss of any of them selects `GeometricOnly` on EVERY target. The time criterion is per target and is the only target-dependent row of the block (D-30). A time miss on one target selects `GeometricOnly` on THAT target only and the other target keeps the tier its own measurement earned, so a wasmJs miss never takes the full solver from desktop and Android (tie-break, stated once: a target never inherits another target's miss). `GeometricOnly` is coincident, horizontal/vertical, equal and distance with `NoConvergence` surfaced, never silent; tangent, symmetric, angle, Fixed, Radius and Parallel stay hidden on that target (the same set as metric 3's fallback). The geometric-only set is re-measured on the missing target against the same time bars; if it misses too, that target is `Hidden` (no constraint panel there; stored constraints are preserved untouched and shown read-only as not available on this device) and the other targets are unaffected; if even the geometric-only set misses the residual bar on every target, the constraint epics (4.x) are deferred and Job 2 stays on the v1 editor. Metric 3 is judged per target on the scenario its tier can pass, the bracket plate on a `Full` target and the "fallback plate" on a `GeometricOnly` target, with every numeric bar unchanged, so the fallback needs no owner amendment of the metric; dropping a numeric bar would still be the owner's decision, not the implementer's.

**Option W keep or drop rule (D-138; the parameters, the cases and the precedence are the blocks below, and `check-plan.py` recomputes every case from the parameters)**: in the OWNER0 session, after Task 0.1.7c has recorded its 5 diagrams, each item carries its **platform**, which is the SteleKit platform the item WOULD be authored on (for an item marked in-app where it will be authored, for an item marked "elsewhere" the platform the owner would author it on if they moved it into SteleKit, recorded for every item, so an "elsewhere" item has a platform too) and, for `web`, its **browser families** (one or more of `chromium`, `firefox`, `safari`). `webItems` is the number of items whose platform is `web` and whose browser families include a counted family (`chromium` or `firefox`): the mark (in-app or elsewhere) does not matter, because Option W is a question of which platform the owner would use, not of how many items would move; an item whose only family is `safari` is NOT counted, because Safari is the least likely to pass the spike and an item that only Safari could serve is not demand Option W can be sure to meet; an item with no recorded platform makes the record incomplete and the rule is not applied until the owner completes it; a `web` item with no recorded browser family counts as having no families, so it is not counted (case 12 of the table below).

# ADR-006: Larger canvas is a view-only pasteboard; points stay clamped to the image

**Status**: Accepted pending owner review (Phase 4 patch, 2026-11-14; was Proposed, revised after review, iteration 2)
**Date**: 2026-11-14
**Project**: sync-lock-and-image-annotator (Epic 2)

## Context

`MIN_VIEW_SCALE = 1f`, `AnnotationViewTransform.scale` defaults to `MIN_VIEW_SCALE` (L57), `isFit` is `scale <= MIN_VIEW_SCALE + FIT_EPSILON` (L61), and `clamped()` returns the identity `Fit` at scale <= 1 (`kmp/src/commonMain/kotlin/dev/stapler/stelekit/ui/annotate/AnnotationViewTransform.kt:13,57,61,109-112`). So "Fit" and "the scale floor" are the same constant in four places. `clampAxis` (L140-145) centres the content and returns a fixed offset whenever it fits the viewport. Annotation points are normalised `[0,1]` and placement clamps to `[0,1]` (`AnnotationPlacement.kt:39,48`, `AnnotationHitTest.kt:65-66`); allowing points outside would put out-of-range values in the sidecar and break undistortion. Export draws from normalised points against the native bitmap and is independent of the view transform (VERIFIED: no `AnnotationViewTransform` reference in the exporter). `hitTestAnnotations` already maps points through the view transform and takes screen-pixel radii (`AnnotationHitTest.kt:19-30`; gesture layer `28 dp`/`20 dp` at L119-120), so handle targets are already constant on screen at any zoom.

## Decision

1. **Decouple Fit from the floor first.** Introduce `FIT_SCALE = 1f`; `AnnotationViewTransform()` defaults to `FIT_SCALE`. **`isFit` and the `clamped()` short-circuit use the symmetric comparison `abs(scale - FIT_SCALE) <= FIT_EPSILON`** (architecture R2): the current one-sided `scale <= MIN_VIEW_SCALE + FIT_EPSILON` (`AnnotationViewTransform.kt:61,111`) would, once the floor is 0.25, report every scale in 0.25..1 as Fit and `clamped()` would snap a 0.25 transform back to the identity, defeating the feature. `toggledAt` (`isFit` -> zoom in, else `Fit`) then does the right thing from 0.5. Then lower `MIN_VIEW_SCALE` to 0.25 (of fit). Tests: at scale 0.5 `isFit` is false and `clamped` preserves 0.5 (centred); at 0.25 it stays 0.25; at `1.0005` it is Fit. A regression test asserts `AnnotationViewTransform()` is Fit and that `Fit` toggles (`toggledAt`) still work from any scale. **The snap must not be sticky (Phase 4 patch, pre-mortem P2-9, adversarial C10):** `transformed()` derives `newScale = (scale * zoom)` from the current, already snapped scale (`AnnotationViewTransform.kt:78-79`) and `clamped()` re-snaps within `FIT_EPSILON` (L109-111), so a slow pinch-out at per-event factors of 0.997-0.999 would re-snap to 1.0 on every event and never leave Fit, a detent the user feels now that the floor is 0.25. The gesture state therefore accumulates the **unsnapped** scale and the snap is applied only for display (`isFit`, `toggledAt`); test: 200 events of factor 0.9995 leave Fit and reach scale about 0.905. The view transform never touches stored measurement values (a separate golden test in Task 3.2c guards the `setGeometry` predicate change that could).
2. **One clamp rule.** Below Fit (`scale < FIT_SCALE`): the image is centred on each axis whose content fits the viewport, pan is disabled on that axis, and the pasteboard absorbs finger reach to the edges. At and above Fit: the offset is clamped so the far edge stays `slack` short of the viewport edge, `slack` proportional to the viewport (25 percent of the dimension), so any pixel can be centred under the loupe (triad repair 2: the Pan toggle and the pan row are therefore available exactly when `scale >= FIT_SCALE`, one rule shared with ux.md 4.1 and UX-53). The old "keeps at least 25 percent of the image visible" statement is dropped: it is vacuous below Fit (centred, fully visible) and the above-Fit rule guarantees visibility by construction. Property tests are scoped accordingly: "point under the pinch centroid stays fixed" holds where the result is unclamped (at or above Fit, or on an axis where content exceeds the viewport); below Fit the property is "zoom is about the viewport centre"; "toCanvas(toView(p)) == p" holds everywhere.
3. The extra canvas is a **pasteboard**: a neutral surface plus a thin image-bounds frame (3:1 against the pasteboard), drawn behind the image. It is never exported and never persisted.
4. Points are still clamped to `[0,1]`. The finger may be in the pasteboard; the point lands on the nearest image edge pixel. Hit-testing uses the raw screen distance and is not clamped. No schema change.
5. **Hit radii are screen dp and stay screen dp.** The earlier "divide hit radius by view scale" idea is dropped: it would make targets shrink or grow with zoom. The unit question is answered ("screen px, dp-derived"); only a property test ("a handle is grabbed within the same screen radius at 0.25 and 1.0") and the 24 dp minimum (UX-24) remain. 
6. **System back-gesture inset (adversarial C11).** On Android the canvas viewport is laid out inside `WindowInsets.systemGestures` left and right (zero on desktop and with three-button navigation), so at Fit the outermost image pixel is never under the screen-edge back-gesture zone and an edge-started drag is not claimed by the system. A press or release outside that inset rectangle is outside the canvas viewport and follows the ADR-007 off-canvas rule. The cost (about 2 x 24-32 dp of a 360 dp width at Fit on gesture navigation) is accepted; zooming out into the pasteboard remains the way to get more reach. Compose `systemGestureExclusion` is not used as the primary fix because Android caps exclusion at 200 dp of height per edge. Owner-verified on the phone (Task 2.7a); the inset value is UNVERIFIED for the owner's device until then.
7. +/-/Fit move to the toolbar's second row at phone widths (rail at 600 dp or more), 48 dp targets, outside the image area; zoom-out is enabled down to the new floor and the percentage is the TalkBack value.
8. 12 MP at scale below 1: decode bounded for scale below Fit if the audit (Task 2.1a) shows the screen draws the full bitmap.

## Consequences

- No sidecar, export or calibration change; risk is limited to the transform and hit-testing, covered by property tests in `commonTest`.
- 12 MP perf at scale below 1 is checked against the 60 fps SLO in Story 2.7 (baseline currently UNVERIFIED).
- Edge reach at exactly Fit relies on zooming out below Fit; that is by design.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Allow points outside [0,1]**: schema, undistortion and export risk for no accuracy gain. Rejected.
- **Only add `slack` at scale >= 1**: gives edge reach but no overview. Insufficient; combined with the lower floor.
- **Pan below Fit**: the image is already fully visible; a free pan adds nothing and breaks the pinch-centroid property. Rejected.
- **Third-party zoomable (Telephoto)**: alpha, would fight the custom gesture layer. Rejected (`research/build-vs-buy.md` 1d).

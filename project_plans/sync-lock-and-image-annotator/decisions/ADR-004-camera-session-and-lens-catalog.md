# ADR-004: One CameraSession and a CameraLensCatalog port own lens binding

**Status**: Accepted pending owner review (Phase 4 patch, 2026-11-14; was Proposed)
**Date**: 2026-11-14
**Project**: sync-lock-and-image-annotator (Epic 4)

## Context

Four sites hard-code `CameraSelector.DEFAULT_BACK_CAMERA` (VERIFIED by grep): `kmp/src/androidMain/kotlin/dev/stapler/stelekit/ui/components/CameraViewfinderDialog.android.kt:108`, `.../platform/sensor/AndroidCameraProvider.kt:116`, `.../platform/sensor/AndroidCameraPreviewBinder.kt:54` and `.../platform/sensor/AndroidCameraFrameSource.kt:175` (an `ImageAnalysis` bound to a lifecycle owner). `unbindAll()` is called by the dialog (L105, L118) and by `AndroidCameraProvider` (L113, L161); the preview binder and the frame source deliberately do not, to compose with another bound use case. CameraX is 1.4.1 and **already exposes physical-camera binding**: `javap` on the resolved `camera-core-1.4.1.aar` and `camera-camera2-1.4.1.aar` shows `CameraSelector.Builder.setPhysicalCameraId(String)`, `Camera2Interop.Extender.setPhysicalCameraId(String)`, `Camera2Interop.Extender.setSessionCaptureCallback(...)` and `Camera2CameraInfo.getCameraCharacteristicsMap()` (physical id to characteristics). The earlier claim that physical binding needs 1.7 RC was wrong (`research/stack.md` already recorded it as present since 1.4.0-alpha05). OEMs may hide ultra-wide and tele behind one logical camera, and the AVD has no multi-camera hardware (`research/pitfalls.md` section 4).

## Decision

1. `CameraLensCatalog` (commonMain port; `CameraLens(id, facing, focalLengthMm?, focal35mmEq?, zoomRatio?, isLogicalMulti, kind)`) and `CameraSession` (androidMain; owns `ProcessCameraProvider`, selector, `Preview`, `ImageCapture`, `Camera`; `bind`, `switchLens`, `setZoom`, `setFlash`, `unbind`). The four binders (dialog, provider, preview binder, frame source) become clients. Lens changes are serialised through one state machine on the main thread, the shutter is disabled while rebinding, results from a previous binding are dropped, and **`CameraSession` itself never calls `unbindAll()`**: it unbinds only its own use cases. **Phase 4 patch (pre-mortem P2-10):** the dialog's own `unbindAll()` (`CameraViewfinderDialog.android.kt:105,118`, VERIFIED by grep this pass; `AndroidCameraProvider.kt:113,161` likewise) moves onto the session's own-use-case unbind (Task 4.4a) and `unbindAll` is gone from the dialog; **every** bind, `CameraX` future and `Camera2Interop` callback registration catches `Throwable` (rethrowing `CancellationException`) because an OEM can throw `IllegalArgumentException`, `SecurityException` or `CameraInfoUnavailableException` (not only `CameraUnavailableException`) and an uncaught `Throwable` kills the process on Android; a throwing bind keeps the previous lens. The `@ExperimentalCamera2Interop` opt-in is scoped to the files that use it. The one remaining `unbindAll()` caller, `AndroidCameraProvider`'s one-shot `capturePhoto` (L113/L161), is scoped to explicit teardown of its own session, and a test asserts that tearing one down leaves the preview binder's and frame source's use cases bound. `AndroidCameraFrameSource` follows the selected lens (analysis on a different lens than the preview would diverge).
2. Enumerate back cameras via `availableCameraInfos` plus `Camera2CameraInfo.getCameraCharacteristicsMap()` (focal lengths per physical id); classify lenses by focal length and sensor size, never by id order. Stay on CameraX 1.4.1, no bump. **Path B (physical ids) is available now**: where `getPhysicalCameraIds()` lists physical cameras behind a logical one, the catalog offers a lens per physical id **only if a bind with `CameraSelector.Builder.setPhysicalCameraId` succeeded in the probe or at runtime** (OEM behaviour varies; Task 4.1a tests it per physical id on the owner's phone and records the result in the fixture). Where bind fails or one logical camera hides lenses, show zoom-ratio chips from `ZoomState` and label only real optical stops as lenses. **Triad repair 1: the implementation is zoom-chip first** (Task 4.3b always produces the zoom stops and the separately exposed back cameras); the physical-id path is UNVERIFIED until the owner's probe run (Task 4.1b) and never assumed, and a phone that hides ultra-wide/tele behind one logical camera still meets SM4 through honestly labelled zoom-ratio chips (requirements SM4 fallback acceptance).
3. The classifier is a pure function over `List<CameraLensInfo>`, tested against recorded `CameraCharacteristics` fixtures from the owner's phone. A one-time capability probe writes ids, focal lengths, capabilities and zoom ranges to the persisted log.
4. Exclude the front camera. A lens that fails to bind keeps the previous lens running, is shown disabled with a reason, and is logged.
5. Never apply `LENS_DISTORTION` to a saved JPEG (measurement-accuracy ADR-001/004). **Lens record mechanism** (the capture path uses `ImageCapture.OnImageSavedCallback`, which carries no `CaptureResult`, VERIFIED at `CameraViewfinderDialog.android.kt:233`): (a) a `Camera2Interop.Extender.setSessionCaptureCallback` on the use-case builder keeps the latest repeating-request `TotalCaptureResult` fields (`LENS_FOCAL_LENGTH`, `LOGICAL_MULTI_CAMERA_ACTIVE_PHYSICAL_ID`, `SCALER_CROP_REGION` / `CONTROL_ZOOM_RATIO`) in an atomic holder, and the snapshot taken at shutter press is the primary record (`source = PREVIEW_RESULT_AT_SHUTTER`); (b) if the probe (Task 4.1b) shows the callback or the active-physical-id key is not delivered, the record falls back to the selected lens's characteristics plus the zoom ratio at shutter (`source = SELECTED_LENS`); (c) the EXIF `FocalLength` read from the saved JPEG **before** `ExifOrientationFixer` (already done: `fixResult.focalLengthMm`) is stored as a cross-check (`exifFocalMm`). The chip choice alone is never the record. **A `SELECTED_LENS`-sourced record names the chip, not the lens that took the photo, so it never auto-selects a `CameraProfile` (Phase 4 patch, pre-mortem P2-11):** only `PREVIEW_RESULT_AT_SHUTTER` records are eligible for import-time profile matching, and only when `exifFocalMm` agrees with the record's `focalLengthMm` within 15 percent; otherwise no profile is chosen and the UI shows the record's `source`. Fields are nullable on `PlatformImageFile` and `ImageSensorData`, persisted in the sidecar only, and consumed at import time by `ImageImportService` to match a `CameraProfile`, so no DB column. `ExifOrientationFixer` re-encodes rotated JPEGs and rewrites only `Orientation` (`ExifOrientationFixer.kt:86-102`), dropping `FocalLength`, `Make`, `Model`, `DateTime`; it also downscales to a 16 MP budget (pre-existing). The fixer copies those tags back after re-encoding (Task 4.5c) and nothing relies on EXIF written before it.
6. `expect fun CameraViewfinderDialog` keeps its signature; results ride on `PlatformImageFile`.

## Consequences

- The physical-lens claim "every rear lens the device exposes" is defined as: every separately bindable back camera plus every optical zoom stop the logical camera exposes. Per-physical-id capture is not promised.
- Device-only behaviour (bind, switch time, hidden lenses) is owner-verified; pure logic is CI-verified.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Raw Camera2**: regression risk across preview, rotation, lifecycle. Rejected.
- **Bump CameraX to 1.6.2 or 1.7 RC now**: 1.4.1 already has the physical-id APIs the plan needs; a bump adds risk without a demonstrated gap. Deferred until the probe shows 1.4.1 cannot reach the owner's lenses.
- **Add lens parameters to the expect dialog signature**: forces churn on every platform actual. Rejected.
- **New DB column for lens fields**: no query needs it; import-time consumption suffices. Rejected.
